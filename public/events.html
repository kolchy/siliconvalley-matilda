Events Calendar

We meet every second Wednesday, 5:00-7:00pm, rotating through four Cremorne venues that can host around 20 people and run happy hour specials. To RSVP or ask a question, email events@kolchy.com.

Our venues

  • The Cherry Tree Hotel

    Corner of Balmain and Stephenson Streets, Cremorne. A classic local pub with a spacious front bar and happy hour specials.

  • The Precinct Hotel

    60 Swan Street, Cremorne. A big, flexible pub on the Richmond/Cremorne border with room for groups of 20 and weekday happy hours.

  • The Terminus Hotel

    Corner of Victoria and Elizabeth Streets, Abbotsford (just north of Cremorne). A renovated corner pub with generous happy hour deals.

  • The Posty

    90 Swan Street, Cremorne. A relaxed bar with share plates and a happy hour that suits an after-work crowd.
